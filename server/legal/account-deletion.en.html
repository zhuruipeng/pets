Delete your account and data

Applies to both the Mainland China and international versions of “My Pet”  ·  Updated: 30 September 2026

This page explains how to close your account, what gets deleted, what cannot be deleted, and how long it takes.

1. Two different things

“My Pet” is local-first. Your pet’s profile, entries, and photos live on your phone; only after you sign in does part of that sync to our servers. So signing out and closing your account are different operations:

OperationWhat happensHow
Sign out All local data is kept, it just stops uploading. Data on the server also stays — sign in again and it comes back. In the App: Me → Data sync → Sign out
Close account The account and its synced data are deleted from the server and cannot be recovered. Self-service deletion also clears this account’s data on the requesting device. See section 2 below

2. How to close your account

In versions supporting self-service deletion, sign in and open My → Data sync → Delete account. Export a backup if needed, read the scope, type DELETE and confirm permanent deletion. An internet connection is required.

If a pet you own still has other active carers, deletion is blocked without changing data. Resolve its sharing first. Pets owned by others that you help care for are retained.

This deletes only the regional Pet App account. It does not close your Chuxiu website account or accounts in other products. Signing in again creates a new empty Pet App account.

If you cannot sign in or use an older version, email zhuruipeng@weiyuantool.com, subject Delete my account, with your sign-in phone or email. We verify account ownership. Do not send passwords or login tokens.

3. What gets deleted

  • The regional server profile, contact details, password hash, all Pet App login tokens and verification codes for its contact targets.
  • Exclusively owned pets, records, expenses, reminders, walks, attachments, cloud photos and historical sync snapshots.
  • Your shared-care membership and pending invitations for exclusively owned pets. Shared care facts on others’ pets remain with structured attribution anonymized.
  • This account’s local records, pending sync writes, cursor, photo-sync setting and app-owned photo, document and avatar copies on the requesting device. Other local accounts and their referenced files remain.

4. What remains

  • Other carers’ shared pets and facts. Anonymizing attribution does not rewrite free-text medical facts or notes. Contact us about personal information in those fields.
  • Offline copies on other devices, gallery originals and exported backups. Remove these separately. An email request cannot remotely erase a phone.
  • Minimal random identifiers and deletion signals, a one-way identity marker preventing offline edits restoring attribution, and a token-hash receipt allowing only deletion retries for 24 hours. These cannot be used to sign in.
  • Restricted operational backups until normal rotation. They are not active business data; completed deletion requests must be reapplied if a backup is restored.

5. How long it takes

Self-service deletion takes effect when the server transaction completes. All devices lose sign-in access immediately. This device clears its account data after receiving confirmation. Temporarily locked app-owned files enter a durable retry queue; cloud authorization has already been removed. If the response is lost, retry on the same screen to safely confirm the result.

We verify emailed requests and reply within 15 working days. Account deletion cannot be undone. Save any necessary backup first.

6. Deleting only part of it

In most cases you do not need to close the whole account:

  • Just one pet’s records → delete them directly in the App (soft-deleted locally, no longer displayed)
  • No longer sharing with someone → remove the member under “Co-care”, or have them leave
  • No longer syncing → sign out; the server data stays untouched
  • No longer want reminders → turn off the relevant reminders in the App, or turn off notification permission in system settings
