## 1. What the App does

The App provides pet health profiles and reminder management:

- Create a pet profile (breed, birthday, weight, neuter status, and so on)
- Record weight, vaccines, deworming, medication, vet visits, feeding, bathroom, and
  other entries, with optional photo attachments
- Generate **suggested schedules** based on your pet's species, age, and the general
  immunisation guidelines for your region
- Notify you when a schedule is due (local notifications)
- Record walk traces and distances
- Sync across multiple devices after you sign in, and invite family members to record
  for the same pet
- Generate a lost-pet poster (an image) for you to share yourself

## 2. Important: reminders are not veterinary advice

**This is the clause in these terms that matters most.**

The vaccine, deworming, and check-up schedules in the App are **suggestions** derived
from **published general immunisation guidelines**. They are not a diagnosis, a
prescription, or a treatment plan for your individual animal. They cannot account for:

- Your pet's medical history, allergies, or current medications
- Recent disease outbreaks in your area
- Individual differences (breed, size, health status)

**Always follow your licensed vet's advice.** If you notice any abnormal symptoms, see a
vet directly rather than relying on this App's reminders.
